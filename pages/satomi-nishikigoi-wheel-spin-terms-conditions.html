Every Wheel has its own koi question, shown on the Wheel's page when the Wheel opens. To enter, paid or free, you must type the correct answer into the box on that page. When your answer is correct, the buttons to take a spot are unlocked. Your answer is saved with your order.

6. The spin

6.1 When

Once all 20 spots are taken, we announce the spin date on WhatsApp and on the Wheel's page, giving at least 24 hours' notice. Spins take place live on twitch.tv/satominishikigoi on the next available Friday or Sunday. We post reminders on WhatsApp 30 minutes and 5 minutes before going live.

6.2 How

  1. All 20 entrants are listed on screen and shuffled 5 times using wheelofnames.com. The order after the 5th shuffle is the order in which entrants take their spin.
  2. The wheel has 20 segments: one marked JACKPOT and 19 numbered 1 to 19.
  3. Each entrant's spin lands on one segment, which is then removed from the wheel. The wheel therefore gets smaller with each spin, and the jackpot is won exactly once in every Wheel.
  4. If your spin lands on JACKPOT, you win the jackpot koi. If it lands on a number, you win a koi worth at least the price of a paid spot on that Wheel.
  5. The whole stream is recorded and uploaded to YouTube, where anyone can watch it back.

6.3 If the stream fails

If the spin can't go ahead or finish live (for example because of an internet, Twitch or power failure, or illness), we will reschedule it, tell all entrants by WhatsApp or email, and post the new date on the Wheel's page. All entries remain valid. This could be the same night.

7. Your chances

Every spot on a Wheel, paid or free, has exactly the same chance: 1 in 20 (5%) of winning the jackpot koi, and a 100% chance of winning a koi. Because the order of spins is shuffled at random and each landed segment is removed, your place in the order makes no difference to your chances.

8. Prizes

8.1 What you can win

Every spot wins a live koi:

  • The jackpot koi is described on the Wheel's page, with its variety, breeder, size and age, plus photos and, where possible, video.
  • Every other spot wins a koi worth at least the price of a paid spot on that Wheel. For example, on a Wheel with £70 paid spots, every non-jackpot spot wins a koi worth at least £70. This applies to the free spot too.

There is no cash alternative.

8.2 If a prize koi becomes unavailable

Koi are live animals. If the jackpot koi becomes unavailable before the spin through illness, injury or another welfare reason, we will either replace it with a koi of equal value and a similar variety, giving notice on the Wheel's page and telling entrants before the spin, or cancel the Wheel and refund all paid spots in full (section 10). We will never replace a prize with a lesser koi.

8.3 The first spot locks the jackpot

While a Wheel is open and before any spot has been sold, the jackpot koi may also be offered for sale in our shop. If it is bought in the shop before any spot is sold, that Wheel is paused, and a new jackpot koi of equal or greater value will be announced before the Wheel reopens.

As soon as the first spot on a Wheel is sold, the jackpot koi is locked to that Wheel. It is withdrawn from sale and will not be sold, swapped or replaced, except as set out in section 8.2.

In the rare event that the jackpot koi and a spot are bought at almost the same moment and the jackpot koi sells first, we will contact the spot-holder within 24 hours and offer a full refund or a spot on the next Wheel.

8.4 Your responsibilities as a winner

You must have a suitable pond, holding facility or arrival tank for a koi of the size described, be available for the agreed delivery or collection, and care for the koi from the moment it reaches you. If, on reasonable enquiry, you can't show you have somewhere suitable to keep it, we will keep the koi for its welfare.
