Why Koi Health Matters

Even though koi are resilient, they require the right water conditions, nutrition, preventative and remedial care to thrive. Poor pond water quality, seasonal temperature shifts, parasites, bacterial infections and stress in general can lead to illness or even loss. Prevention , early detection and intervention are crucial for maintaining a healthy koi pond.
