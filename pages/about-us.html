A bit about me. I got into the fish keeping hobby at the age of 6. Like many I grew up with 8 large fish tanks in our family 3 bed terrace, my Dad was addicted. I grew up learning from my Dad and to say I’m just as addicted is an understatement. Because of this although I’m only 29 I have been keeping fish for 20+ years. When I was 6 I had a small 40 litre tank I cared for myself and water changed with a jug! My hobby went from Goldfish to Stingrays, Discus, Arowana and ponds. I have now set out on a childhood dream of having my own Koi shop and applying the knowledge I’ve gained over the years to help people succeed in the hobby. Satomi Nishikigoi will supply koi to meet all budgets and offer ways to obtain high quality koi with a smaller budget. One thing I pride myself on is after care, after all I have been a hobbyist for a long time there’s nothing better than being able to trust your supplier and be looked after in every way. There will be no hairs and grazes about Satomi Nishikigoi whether you spend £500 or £5 every customer will have my support for life.

Joshua Whipday
